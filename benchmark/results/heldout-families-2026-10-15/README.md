# Held-out family and size study

This is a prospectively declared generalization study using the exact frozen compressed-query candidate. **No native performance capture has been released or executed for this study.** The matrix and analysis rule were prepared before the parent compressed-query timing completed; "held out" refers to new fixed realizations and this matrix, not previously unexamined family names.

The [protocol](PROTOCOL.md) and [ordered manifest](manifest.csv) fix six existing families (`leaf_distance4`/`leaf_shuffled4`, `grid2d_metric`/`grid2d_random`, `line_metric`/`line_random`) at six vertex counts from 1,024 through 1,048,576, density 32, two graph seeds and one scalar sampling seed. The 72-child campaign would produce 3,024 certified outputs, retaining all six solvers, both clocks, every loss and all endpoints. No parent binary rebuild or historical timing pooling is planned.

The collector and tests are [run_heldout_families.py](../../run_heldout_families.py) and [test_heldout_families.py](../../test_heldout_families.py). Both the owner and independent reviewer passed all **nine mocked groups** on their first attempts; exact source hashes, commands, logs, statuses and known-empty process-group evidence are in [owner-mocks-attempt01.json](validation/owner-mocks-attempt01.json) and [independent-mocks-attempt01.json](validation/independent-mocks-attempt01.json). The independent installed-source review also passed. No native solver ran in those tests. Preflight and a committed freeze are still required before performance release. The [seed check](validation/seed-nonreuse-check.json) found no reuse in earlier local source/protocol/freeze configurations within its stated scope.

The analysis retains 4,320 individual clock-specific KKT/comparator pairs, 360 case and 720 graph comparison summaries, 432 case and 864 graph absolute-time summaries, 180 point classifications, all 450 measured endpoint pairs and 30 family/comparator summaries. Exact rational graph medians determine a strong bracket: loss at an earlier measured point, at least 10% advantage at a later point on both graphs and clocks, and the same strong lead at every still-larger measured point. It does not interpolate a crossing or infer asymptotic dominance.

The original [source preparation note](SOURCE_PREPARATION.md) retains the planned CLI and clearly identifies its historical unexecuted status. Execute preflight only after validation, then commit its freeze before a separate quiet-performance release. This README is preparation status, not a benchmark result.
