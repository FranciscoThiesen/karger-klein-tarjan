# Eighth-only hierarchy size extension — not executed

This is a prospective source package declared before reading the paired
eighth-sampling study's results. **No imports, tests, preflight, freeze, solver
calls or timings have been performed for this extension.** Root will decide
whether to release it after the complete paired study is validated. A decision
not to run leaves this declaration unexecuted rather than making it a result.

The [protocol](PROTOCOL.md), [literal manifest](manifest.csv) and
[seed scan](SEED_FRESHNESS.md) fix two weight twins over seven sizes: 1,024 to
4,194,304 vertices, all m/n=32, two fresh graph seeds and one sampler seed.
The exact frozen eighth candidate and all five controls remain unchanged.
The preparation contains a copied, locally adapted strict collector and eleven
mocked test groups; all execution and numerical/visual validation are deferred.

The planned 28 children produce 1,176 certified solver outputs. All five direct
comparisons, both clocks, two graph medians, absolute times and losses will be
retained. The sustained crossover rule keeps all 70 size classifications and
210 earlier/later pairs. It requires a smaller measured loss, a later lead of
at least 10%, and that lead at every still-larger measured size. It does not
interpolate a threshold or establish asymptotic dominance.

The lower endpoint is a nearly complete 256-vertex core with average degree
250; the larger cores have the same average degree and lower edge density as
a fraction of all possible pairs. These engineered families test the role of
weight order, not the prevalence of such behavior in natural graphs.

Results, resource outcomes, independent audit, plots and all ten family/control
conclusions remain **pending**. This single-binary experiment cannot estimate
the effect of switching half sampling to eighth sampling; it cannot pool the
earlier paired timings to manufacture that comparison.
