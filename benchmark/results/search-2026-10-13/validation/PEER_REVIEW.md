# Capture and analysis peer review

The native harness, serial capture wrapper and analyzer were independently reviewed before campaign timing. The review checked the five-algorithm rotation, retained warmups, exact child matrix and combined-CSV concatenation, independent-certificate marker, input/control fingerprints, unchanged public timing boundaries, resource ledger, and raw/source/binary provenance records. No unresolved defect was found in these paths.

One review finding was corrected before capture: UTC timestamps initially served as strict child-order gates despite the promise to retain clock corrections. The wrapper now records monotonic start/end timestamps for child/capture order; the analyzer validates those intervals and keeps UTC reversals as descriptive flags. The owner's synthetic full-capture test covers that case.

The independent [stdlib semantic test](peer_semantic_check.py) and [result](peer_semantic_check.json) use 270 synthetic rows with three graph seeds, three sampling seeds and all five positions. A deliberately skewed example gives a hierarchical paired wall ratio of 6 while pooling individual ratios gives 4; the analyzer returns 6. The corresponding CPU ratio is independently 6.75. All 360 metric/comparator pairs and all win/loss/tie counts remain present. A synthetic sleep-affected call and reversed UTC call are retained and flagged. No native solver or measured call ran in this peer test.

The outputs support descriptive comparisons for the captured graph/seed matrix and build. Sampling seeds do not create additional graph samples; ranges are not confidence intervals. The five-position rotation balances positions, not every possible preceding-algorithm pair. Periodic process-group RSS is a sampled ceiling check rather than a continuous memory bound. The analyzer retains claimed build/source inventories and raw hashes; those records complement the archived native build checks, rather than proving a compiler command from executable bytes alone. Any crossover or fresh-seed confirmation claim still requires the separately frozen campaign protocol and complete results.
