The wrapper uses a **21,600-second (six-hour) whole-run ceiling**, not an expected runtime and not a per-child wall deadline. Its interval includes launch, bounded cleanup, strict reload and log hashing. Cleanup has a bounded 30-second TERM grace and five-second KILL grace; if cleanup crosses the overall ceiling, the attempt fails. The frozen child collector still enforces its existing sampled 40 GiB resource ceiling and empty-group checks, without a total per-child wall deadline. The outer sampler observes the Python collector's process group, not detached native child groups.

The raw destination is fixed as `distance.csv`, `distance.json`, and `distance_cases/`; any existing destination, including rejected captures, is preserved and prevents another launch. Each outer attempt has an exclusive directory under study `validation/`. Exit status is the actual observed collector status, never synthesized from a partial sidecar. Success requires actual zero exit, known-empty outer group, complete strict reload of all 24 native groups and 1,008 solver rows, source/input rechecks, and completion inside the overall ceiling. Failed or interrupted strict proof leaves detached native state conservatively unknown, even when the Python group is known empty.

After successful completion, run `machine_power.py --phase after` through the nonnative guard. Strict analysis/audit/plotting follow the study preparation instructions. A failed power query is retained and does not become evidence of no sleep. Original-resolution and final publication visual QA are separate later gates.
