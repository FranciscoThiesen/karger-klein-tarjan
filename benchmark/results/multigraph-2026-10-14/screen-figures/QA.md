# Figure inspection

Both generated PNGs were visually inspected after rendering. All ten cases,
both clocks, labels, ratio directions, full trial/graph ranges, comparator
losses, captions and the common heatmap scale are visible without clipping.
The large ordered implementation ratios round to 0.48; their compactor ratios
round to 0.40, while Kruskal/Prim losses remain visible. SVG/PDF exports use
the same figures but were not separately rendered for inspection.

`provenance.json` records source and output hashes; this note completes its
initially pending visual review.
