# Figure review

All ten PNG figures were visually reviewed at original resolution after generation:
`variant_gains_wall`, `variant_gains_cpu`, and the `packed`/`scalar` ×
`ratios`/`times` × `wall`/`cpu` figures. Labels, legends, endpoint ticks,
reference lines, realization markers and captions are readable and not clipped.

The generation receipt separately verifies all 30 panels, complete point ranges
and matched wall/CPU axes. Figures retain both variants, all six solvers, both
clocks, all trials and all four graph/sampler realizations. Lines connect measured
endpoints; they do not estimate a crossing location. Trial ranges are descriptive,
not confidence intervals. No control normalization or historical pooling is used.

The original generation receipt's pending visual-review field is preserved as
historical provenance. This file records the subsequent completed visual review.
